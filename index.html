About

I work on efficient AI: making large models smaller, faster, and more energy-efficient through model compression and brain-inspired computation.

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), advised by Prof. Malu Zhang, and part of a joint Ph.D. program with the Shenzhen Loop Area Institute. I received my B.Eng. from UESTC. My research focuses on model compression, combining large models with spiking neural networks, and neuromorphic computing.
