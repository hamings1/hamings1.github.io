BSO: Binary Spiking Online Optimization Algorithm

Online optimization for binary weights with less training memory.

Yu Liang, Yu Yang, Wenjie Wei, Ammar Belatreche, Shuai Wang, Malu Zhang, Yang Yang

Read paper ↗Code ↗BibTeX

Overview

BSO reduces the memory burden of training binary spiking networks by updating binary weights directly. Its temporal-aware variant, T-BSO, uses gradient information across time steps to adjust the update threshold.

How it works

01

Direct binary updates

Flip a binary weight when the product of gradient momentum and the weight crosses a threshold, removing the need to maintain latent weights.

02

Temporal-aware thresholds

T-BSO incorporates temporal gradient information into adaptive threshold selection.

Key results

No latent weights

direct weight updates within an online training framework

Convergence analysis

formal regret bounds for BSO and T-BSO

The paper reports improved optimization performance over the evaluated binary spiking training methods. Consult its experiments for accuracy and memory comparisons.
